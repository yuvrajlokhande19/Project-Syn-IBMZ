2. THE ARCHITECTURE (THE 3 PILLARS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 1: Trusted Telemetry (The Firewall)
Fake hospital sensors generate data and sign it with a secret HMAC key. If a 
hacker modifies the data, the signature breaks, and our backend drops it 
before the AI even sees it.

PILLAR 2: Safety-Bounded Composite AI
- Snap ML (Random Forest): Runs natively on s390x to detect anomalies.
- Deterministic Logic: A hard-coded algorithm makes the life-or-death 
  ambulance routing decision (NOT the AI).
- Big-Endian Phi-3 LLM: Translates the final decision into Hindi/Marathi. 
  It acts only as a translator, never a decision-maker.

PILLAR 3: Tamper-Evident Audit Chain
Every accepted payload and decision is chained via HMAC-SHA256 into an SQLite 
database, simulating IBM Z's native CPACF cryptographic hardware acceleration.
